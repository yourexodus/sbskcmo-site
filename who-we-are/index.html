About Us

Contact

We are your typical American family. We celebrate all major holidays and teach our kids 3 main family values: love God, love your family, and love your neighbor. As parents, we pride ourselves in supporting each other and creating a nurturing environment for our kids.

This is much easier done with financial security and emotional stability. We do not take it for granted because it has not always been the case.

We understand the challenges that come with the loss of employment, loss of transportation, or traumatic injury. That is why we created Small Business Solutions to show we care and we understand.

Here is a way we can help our community build financial credibility while maintaining stable housing. We believe if we can create stability in our community, parents can focus on what is most important—their family.

By sharing our values with our neighbors, we can create stronger and safer communities. We believe by creating programs that meet basic needs and build personal growth, we will create a better place for us all to live.
